# Credits for the store screenshot art

The sleeves and photos in `art/` come from Wikimedia Commons. Each is used under
the licence its own Commons page gives; most of the sleeves were released there
by the artist or label with a permission ticket on file (VRTS). Titles, artists
and track lists in `StoreData.js` are the real releases', taken from MusicBrainz.

All files were cropped to a square, scaled down (sleeves to at most 640 px,
photos to at most 400 px), converted to sRGB and saved as JPEG without metadata.
Those are the only changes. The changed files are under the same licence as
their source, as CC BY-SA asks.

Anyone who publishes a screenshot that shows one of these images should keep the
attribution with it, for example by linking to this file.
