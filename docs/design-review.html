The mark

Three identical parallel bands, filled rather than stroked, so nothing shows a rounded line end. Solving 7W + 2A = 64 makes the band thickness, the blue gaps and the top and bottom margins all exactly equal. The half period is now 32 units rather than 16: a vertically offset band is only cos(slope) as thick as at the crests, so at the old 46° the white looked visibly thinner where it crossed. At 25° the variation is about 9% and stops reading. Stretched that way three bands still survive 16px, so there is one mark at every size and no separate tray asset.

Themes

Four dark and two light. Every colour moves into a token, so a theme is one palette swap and nothing keeps a hardcoded blue. Each mock shows the sidebar, a page and the player bar in that palette. The name under each is what appears in Settings.

Settled, and these are the six that shipped. Graphite is gone: you called the violet accent sloppy, so the fourth dark theme is Forest, a green accent on green-grey neutrals. Daylight and Paper went in as drafted, Paper warm with the green accent. The two light themes were the real work, as expected: about forty places in the QML assumed a dark ground, including white-on-cover text, the overlay gradients on the page heroes and the Qt.rgba(1,1,1,0.04) hover fills. Measuring the contrast instead of judging it by eye caught two things the swatch strips hid: white on the accent fails on every dark theme, and Paper had its ground and its surface the wrong way round, so cards sat lighter than the page they were on. Both are fixed, and both now have a test holding them in place.

Tokens

The palettes are tested rather than eyeballed: 43 cases check WCAG contrast for every token against each of the three grounds it is drawn on (bg, surface, surfaceHigh), that the elevation ramp moves away from the ground in whichever direction away is for that theme, that the translucent tokens really are translucent and really are on the accent's hue, and that no token is present in one palette and missing from another. Thresholds: textPrimary 7:1, textSec 4.5:1, textDim 2:1, accent 3:1, and ink on a solid fill 4:1.

Corner radius

Today almost everything is radius: 8, with ad-hoc 3, 4, 6, 12, 16 and 20 scattered around. One radius on everything is exactly the default look you wanted gone. This scale gives the radius a job: the rounder something is, the more it reads as a floating control rather than a surface.

Sidebar

Full width, the compact rail, and the rail hover-expanded over the page the way compact mode works in Zen. It is the same sidebar in all three, just with the finder and the library hidden when compact. No toggle button: the width decides. Chips stay icon-only at every width; the labels moved to tooltips.
